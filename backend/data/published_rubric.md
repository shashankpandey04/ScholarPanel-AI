# Global Excellence and Opportunity Scholarship Rubric

[RUBRIC-L01] Published Scoring Guidelines and Evaluation Protocol for Committee Review.
[RUBRIC-L02] Purpose: Transparent, equitable, and rigorous evaluation of grant and scholarship applications.
[RUBRIC-L03] All applicant files must be processed through four structured stages: Intake, Gatekeeper Eligibility, Parallel Merit & Need Scoring, and Panel Synthesis.

## Section 1: Hard Eligibility Criteria (Gatekeeper Screening)
[RUBRIC-L04] Criterion 1.1 (Academic Benchmark): Applicant must possess a cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA) of at least 3.00 on a standard 4.00 scale (or equivalent >= 75%). Applications with GPA < 3.00 are disqualified outright without proceeding to merit or financial scoring.
[RUBRIC-L05] Criterion 1.2 (Enrollment Status): Applicant must maintain active, verified full-time enrollment in an accredited degree program (undergraduate or graduate) for the upcoming academic cycle. Part-time or non-degree enrollment is ineligible.
[RUBRIC-L06] Criterion 1.3 (Residency & Citizenship Status): Applicant must be a lawful permanent resident, citizen, or documented eligible visa holder with confirmed legal study rights in the host jurisdiction. Unverified or ineligible immigration/residency status results in immediate disqualification.
[RUBRIC-L07] Criterion 1.4 (Household Income Ceiling): Total verified annual household income must not exceed the program cap of $85,000 USD (or equivalent INR 12,00,000 / ₹12 Lakhs per annum). Applications exceeding this verified threshold are disqualified under the mission charter.
[RUBRIC-L08] Criterion 1.5 (Academic Integrity): Applicant must have zero documented honor code, plagiarism, or major disciplinary violations on official institutional records.
[RUBRIC-L09] Eligibility Outcome Rule: If any Section 1 condition fails, the application is assigned status REJECT with immediate short-circuiting, citing the exact failed rubric rule and line.

## Section 2: Academic & Achievement Merit Criteria (Total 100 Points, Weight: 55%)
[RUBRIC-L10] Merit evaluation assesses academic distinction, research initiative, extracurricular leadership, and statement of purpose.
[RUBRIC-L11] Criterion 2.1 (GPA & Academic Rigor - Band A, 35-40 pts): Cumulative GPA 3.85–4.00 with rigorous honors, advanced coursework, or exceptional academic standing at an accredited institution.
[RUBRIC-L12] Criterion 2.1 (GPA & Academic Rigor - Band B, 28-34 pts): Cumulative GPA 3.50–3.84 with solid coursework completion, consistent academic performance, and demonstrated foundational mastery.
[RUBRIC-L13] Criterion 2.1 (GPA & Academic Rigor - Band C, 18-27 pts): Cumulative GPA 3.20–3.49 meeting baseline expectations with moderate academic rigor across curriculum.
[RUBRIC-L14] Criterion 2.1 (GPA & Academic Rigor - Band D, 0-17 pts): Cumulative GPA 3.00–3.19 meeting only minimum threshold, showing uneven grades or limited curriculum challenge.
[RUBRIC-L15] Criterion 2.1 Max Score: 40 points total allocation for GPA & Academic Rigor.
[RUBRIC-L16] Criterion 2.2 (Research, Projects & Leadership Impact - Band A, 28-35 pts): Peer-reviewed publications, patented innovations, notable open-source contributions, competitive fellowships, or significant community leadership with documented measurable impact.
[RUBRIC-L17] Criterion 2.2 (Research, Projects & Leadership Impact - Band B, 20-27 pts): Active participation in university research laboratories, leadership of student/community organizations, or recognized capstone engineering/arts projects.
[RUBRIC-L18] Criterion 2.2 (Research, Projects & Leadership Impact - Band C, 10-19 pts): Moderate involvement in departmental clubs, team projects, or informal community volunteer initiatives without executive leadership roles.
[RUBRIC-L19] Criterion 2.2 (Research, Projects & Leadership Impact - Band D, 0-9 pts): Minimal extracurricular participation or lack of verifiable projects beyond standard classroom requirements.
[RUBRIC-L20] Criterion 2.2 Max Score: 35 points total allocation for Research, Projects & Leadership Impact.
[RUBRIC-L21] Criterion 2.3 (Personal Statement Clarity & Goals - Band A, 21-25 pts): Compelling, deeply articulated narrative connecting past challenges, current scholarly pursuits, and concrete societal contributions; exceptional clarity and vision.
[RUBRIC-L22] Criterion 2.3 (Personal Statement Clarity & Goals - Band B, 15-20 pts): Well-structured essay with clear scholastic and career targets, logical coherence, and strong self-awareness.
[RUBRIC-L23] Criterion 2.3 (Personal Statement Clarity & Goals - Band C, 8-14 pts): General statement outlining standard career intentions, lacking detailed milestone roadmaps or distinctive personal insights.
[RUBRIC-L24] Criterion 2.3 (Personal Statement Clarity & Goals - Band D, 0-7 pts): Vague, generic, or poorly articulated statement with weak alignment to educational goals or excessive errors.
[RUBRIC-L25] Criterion 2.3 Max Score: 25 points total allocation for Personal Statement Clarity & Goals.
[RUBRIC-L26] Section 2 Subtotal Formula: Merit Subtotal = (Score 2.1) + (Score 2.2) + (Score 2.3) on a 0 to 100 integer scale.

## Section 3: Financial Need Criteria (Total 100 Points, Weight: 45%)
[RUBRIC-L27] Financial Need evaluation measures economic hardship, family dependent load, and barrier severity to ensure equitable resource allocation.
[RUBRIC-L28] Criterion 3.1 (Verified Household Income & Dependents Ratio - Band A, 50-60 pts): Acute economic hardship. Annual household income <= $30,000 USD (or <= ₹4,00,000 INR) with 3+ dependents, or income per capita <= $7,500 USD (<= ₹1,00,000 INR).
[RUBRIC-L29] Criterion 3.1 (Verified Household Income & Dependents Ratio - Band B, 36-49 pts): High economic need. Annual household income $30,001–$55,000 USD (₹4,00,001–₹7,50,000 INR) with 2+ dependents, resulting in significant tuition burden.
[RUBRIC-L30] Criterion 3.1 (Verified Household Income & Dependents Ratio - Band C, 20-35 pts): Moderate economic need. Annual household income $55,001–$70,000 USD (₹7,50,001–₹10,00,000 INR) with manageable or single dependent obligations.
[RUBRIC-L31] Criterion 3.1 (Verified Household Income & Dependents Ratio - Band D, 0-19 pts): Low financial need. Annual household income $70,001–$85,000 USD (₹10,00,001–₹12,00,000 INR) with minimal dependent constraints.
[RUBRIC-L32] Criterion 3.1 Max Score: 60 points total allocation for Household Income & Dependents Ratio.
[RUBRIC-L33] Criterion 3.2 (Special Circumstances / First-Generation / Unmet Tuition Gap - Band A, 32-40 pts): Documented first-generation college student status, catastrophic medical expenses, displacement/refugee background, sole wage-earner responsibility, or unmet institutional tuition gap > $15,000 USD (> ₹6,00,000 INR).
[RUBRIC-L34] Criterion 3.2 (Special Circumstances / First-Generation / Unmet Tuition Gap - Band B, 22-31 pts): First-generation status with standard tuition shortfall, or non-traditional student managing dependent care alongside studies.
[RUBRIC-L35] Criterion 3.2 (Special Circumstances / First-Generation / Unmet Tuition Gap - Band C, 10-21 pts): Modest unmet financial aid gap ($5,000–$10,000 USD / ₹1,50,000–₹3,00,000 INR) without acute compounding circumstances.
[RUBRIC-L36] Criterion 3.2 (Special Circumstances / First-Generation / Unmet Tuition Gap - Band D, 0-9 pts): Adequate existing financial aid package, no adverse family circumstances, or tuition fully covered by other bursaries.
[RUBRIC-L37] Criterion 3.2 Max Score: 40 points total allocation for Special Circumstances, First-Gen, and Unmet Gap.
[RUBRIC-L38] Section 3 Subtotal Formula: Need Subtotal = (Score 3.1) + (Score 3.2) on a 0 to 100 integer scale.

## Section 4: Panel Weights, Decision Thresholds & Synthesis
[RUBRIC-L39] The evaluation committee requires deterministic aggregation of merit and need scores to prevent arbitrary decision bias.
[RUBRIC-L40] Weighted Total Formula: Weighted Total = (Merit Subtotal * 0.55) + (Need Subtotal * 0.45). Merit weight is fixed at 55% (0.55); Need weight is fixed at 45% (0.45).
[RUBRIC-L41] Decision AWARD: Weighted Total >= 78.0 points. Candidate receives full scholarship grant funding and formal fellowship honors.
[RUBRIC-L42] Decision WAITLIST: Weighted Total between 60.0 and 77.9 points. Candidate is ranked on the competitive waitlist for secondary funding rounds.
[RUBRIC-L43] Decision REJECT: Weighted Total < 60.0 points OR any failed Section 1 Hard Eligibility Criterion. Candidate is not eligible for scholarship allocation in this cycle.
[RUBRIC-L44] Audit Requirement: Every decision must provide line citations for all sub-criteria, transparent score justifications, and a deterministic calculation record.
